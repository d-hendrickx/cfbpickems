Privacy

IRB Pick 'Ems (irbfootball.com) · last updated 2026-09-17

IRB Pick 'Ems is a private college football pick'em app for a small league of friends. This page says plainly what it stores and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email address, used only to sign you in and to match you to your league record. We never see your Google password.
  • What you do in the league: your picks, tiebreaker and Extra Point guesses, chat messages and reactions, league nickname, initials, and alma mater.
  • Optional contact details you enter yourself, such as a phone number for reminders.
  • Notification preferences and, if you turn on push notifications, a device token issued by our push provider (OneSignal).

We do not collect payment information, location, contacts, or anything from your Google account beyond your name and email.

Who can see it

  • Members of your league see your name, picks (only after games go live), records, chat, standings, and your league profile (nickname, initials, alma mater, and notification and display preferences). Nobody in your league, including the commissioner, can see another player's picks while a week is still open.
  • Your league's commissioner can see your email address, and any phone number you provide, in order to manage membership.
  • The app's administrator (the person who runs the service) can see league data to fix problems.

Your information is never sold, shared with advertisers, or shown to people outside your league.

Where it lives

League data is stored in a hosted database (Supabase) and, during the transition from the previous system, in a private Google Sheet. Live scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard; no personal data is sent to ESPN. Push notifications are delivered through OneSignal, which receives only a device token and the notification text.

How long we keep it

League history (picks, results, standings, chat) is kept for as long as the league exists, because it is the record of the competition. If you leave a league, your history stays in that league's record but your account is no longer linked to it.

Your choices

  • You can edit your league nickname, initials, alma mater, and notification settings in the app at any time.
  • You can turn push notifications off on your device or in the app.
  • You can ask the commissioner to unlink your account, or ask the administrator to delete your account and personal details. Email the address below.
